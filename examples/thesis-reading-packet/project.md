- `project_name`: Demo Thesis Reading Packet

- `primary_goal`: turn one argument-first policy brief into a durable first packet
- `current_frontier`: separate thesis, supporting claims, and open evidence pressure points after the initial pass

- `in_scope_sources`: `samples/open/policy-brief-sample.md`
- `out_of_scope_sources`: all other samples and all private BYOS material
