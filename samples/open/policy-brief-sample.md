# Policy Brief Sample

This file is a tiny open sample used to show a clearly non-finance path through `Learning OS`.

## Topic

A city library system should be treated as continuity infrastructure for civic learning, not only as a cultural amenity.

## Thesis

If a city treats library access as continuity infrastructure, it should track whether people can resume reading, research, and job-search activity after interruptions instead of only counting door traffic.

## Claims

- continuity is a different policy target from short-term usage
- institutions need explicit resumption metrics, not only attendance metrics
- a good reading packet should separate recommendation, evidence, and unresolved uncertainty

## Why This Sample Exists

- to give the public repo a clearly non-finance source
- to show thesis-style reading on policy material rather than harness-meta text
- to keep the example public-safe and BYOS-compatible
