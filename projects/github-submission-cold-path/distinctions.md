# Distinctions

- `hot study lane` vs `cold submission lane`
  The hot lane owns source-bound learning packets. The cold lane owns public packaging, release-safe claims, and GitHub-facing state.
- `repo identity` vs `repo marketing`
  Identity is the durable statement of what the repo is and is not. Marketing is how that identity is presented to attract the right users without changing the contract.
- `human entrypoint` vs `AI entrypoint`
  `README.md` and `README.zh-CN.md` are for human orientation. `AI_CONTEXT.md` and `ai-context.json` are for low-ambiguity AI onboarding.
- `public-safe example` vs `private source packet`
  Public examples demonstrate packet shape. Private packets may use local BYOS materials and stay outside tracked history.
- `cold-path project` vs `one-off release tweak`
  If a GitHub-facing change would matter again in a month, it belongs here instead of only in chat memory.
